  1. Fixed time. Thirty simulation steps per second. View speed changes playback pacing, never the calculations.
  2. Invented mechanics. Vehicles circulate, emit abstract pulses, lose shield on impact, and stop at zero shield. All constants are arcade choices without real-world calibration.
  3. Bounded matches. An 80-second cap prevents endless rounds. At the cap, the higher remaining shield share wins; equal shares draw.
  4. Checkable replays. Export any point in a match. Importing it recomputes from its seed and rejects changed outcomes or event records.
